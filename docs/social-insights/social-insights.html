Social Insights

What changed in this redesign, and why

The current page tries to be a home for every person and every question at once. This version answers one question per tab and nothing else. Everything below the fold today (data freshness, how we counted, what we cannot tell yet, staff-only definitions) moves into an info icon or disappears.

Removed

  • See it as: Everyone, Glen's Home, Vidura's Home, an editor's Home, a shoot page, an agency. Social Insights is performance. Personal homes and shoots live elsewhere.
  • Channels tab. Overview already lists every channel; Posts already filters by channel.
  • Money, renamed Revenue.
  • Impressions, What changed, What the numbers say, Same post different place, Lead flows, How we'll know this works, What we can't tell yet, How we counted as page sections. Their useful parts moved into the tabs below; the rest is an info icon.

The eight tabs

  • Overview: every channel we own, active or not, last posted, with its numbers.
  • Posts: what went out, per channel, against the channel's typical post.
  • What works: key learnings by channel, this week and this month.
  • Library: inspiration from competitors and viral ideas to try, not our own posts.
  • Leaderboard: channels ranked on impressions, engagement, leads, revenue.
  • Campaigns: per campaign, posts out by format and how each did.
  • Revenue: total social revenue, then Mindvalley vs Vishen, then by channel.
  • Agencies: per agency, the KPIs for its platform.

Rules the page follows

  • One number per tile, with the change against the period before. No paragraphs under tiles.
  • Platform counts are never added across platforms. Views stay per channel; only revenue and leads total.
  • Every chart has a scale and a comparison line. Dots are posts, lines are the typical post.
  • Not connected is a grey card with a one-line reason, not a text block.
  • Example figures are marked until the source is connected.
