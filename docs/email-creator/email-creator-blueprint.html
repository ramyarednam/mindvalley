How the Email Creator should work

From calendar row to measured sequence, without a form

Today a person opens New sequence, fills a brief, and presses Start with Research. In 2.0 the Official Calendar is the brief, the Comms Calendar is the plan, Email Studio is the review desk, and Braze is the sender. People approve. Everything else runs.

Worked example throughout: AI for Founders Summit · 30 Oct – 1 Nov 2026, taken from the real calendar exports you shared on 27 Oct 2026.

Preview: Tools change every six months. Something else doesn't. · Alternatives: “The wrong way to catch up on AI” · “What I'd tell a founder who feels behind”

Last Tuesday a founder told me she had bought four AI courses this year.

She finished none of them. Not because she is lazy. She runs a company. Her days were already full before anyone said the word “prompt”.

And every month there is a new tool, a new model, a new thread telling her she is falling behind.

Here is what I told her.

Stop trying to learn AI.

The tools change every six months. What does not change is how you think. When you start every decision with the question “what would this look like if AI did the first 80%?”, the tool matters a lot less. You stop collecting courses. You start getting evenings back.

That one shift is what the AI for Founders Summit is built around. Three days, 30 October to 1 November. Free. Live.

On day one I will walk through the exact way I run Mindvalley with this question, including the parts I got wrong first.

If you have ever felt behind, this is the invitation I wish someone had sent me.

Save my free seat

Vishen

P.S. No replays. No recordings. The content moves too fast to be watched later. Your seat is for the live days only.

Baseline & target

  • Lookback 5 years → 3 → 2: first window with at least 2 comparable campaigns.
  • Match: event type → pathway / product → free or paid → list mix.
  • Best = revenue ÷ own target; tie-break revenue per email.
  • Target = max(calendar $ Rev target, baseline × 1.30). Both shown.
  • Baseline's email-by-email subjects, days, lists and rates become reference rows and drafting examples.

Drafting guardrails Mindvalley email skill

  • One belief per sequence. One CTA per email. 250–350 words pre-event.
  • Julie's four stages: Seen → Hope → Trust → Committed, mapped to invite position.
  • Never: “productivity”, fear of job loss, jargon, “pitch”, “We're excited to announce”, multiple CTAs.
  • Always: “invitation”, outcomes before mechanisms, “No replays. No recordings.” from the 48-hour email on.
  • Subject line must pass the text-a-friend test.
  • Comms Calendar: ✋ No Email Day blocks all lists; newsletter day counts 1 on Daily; a note containing “no email” is not a slot.
UTM engine

One Links card per campaign, every link derived

Mirrors the launch manager's four inputs from the UTM tracking sheet (LP, video/blog, SP, other) and adds a launch code. Email is the first consumer; the social set from the sheet comes from the same card so the sheet can retire.

Try it: change a field and the table rebuilds.
Proposed email convention: utm_source=email · utm_medium=<list> · utm_campaign=<code> · utm_content=<seq>_e<n> · utm_term=<segment>. The sheet's social values (mv_fb, vl_ig_story, mv_yt_card…) stay as utm_source for social. Confirm in question 5.
