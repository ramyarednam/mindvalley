The whole email process

From calendar row to measured sequence

Purple steps are where a person acts. Everything else runs in the backend and re-runs when the Official Calendar or the Comms Calendar changes.

① Watch
Calendar Watcher
An Official Calendar row gets a Key Message Doc or Launch Plan → the campaign is detected. Comms Calendar slots are read for it.
② Brief
Brief Builder
Every link in those cells is fetched, plus the Event Type row. Target comes from the launch plan, not the calendar.
③ Research
Existing step
Content Studio research runs on the brief. Unchanged.
④ Baseline
Baseline Finder
Best comparable past campaign from Metabase, 5y → 3y → 2y. Flags when the calendar figure is below baseline.
⑤ Plan · approve
Sequence Planner
Emails on parked days, per list caps, segments proposed, conflicts flagged. Ramya approves in Planning.
⑥ Draft
Drafting Engine
Every email written with the rules and the baseline's matching email; links from the approved UTM Engine card.
⑦ Review · approve
Editor
Edit in place, dictate or talk to the email (Claude or ChatGPT), versions with who edited, approve. Approval triggers DE and ES translation.
⑧ Schedule
Push to Braze as drafts
Copy only. Campaign drafts and canvas drafts go to Braze; the team schedules there.
⑨ Performance
Tracker
Email by email against the baseline's matching email, plus total campaign revenue. Learnings go to Rules for approval.
