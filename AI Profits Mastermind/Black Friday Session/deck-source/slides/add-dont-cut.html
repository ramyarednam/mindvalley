02 · Offer framing

Add value. Don’t cut price.

Protect the price. Stack the value.

Same math

“Buy 1, get 2 free” = “30% off 3.” The first one sells.

Instead of

“$2,000 program, now 50% off”

Say

“$2,000 program + 3 private calls + my AI toolkit. This week only.”

Black Friday Launch Engine · Source: Alex Hormozi, $100M Offers · 14

Slide 14 · starts 24:30 · full words in the script doc. Key points: Alex Hormozi’s point: identical math, very different feeling. Free stuff feels bigger than a percentage. For anything high-ticket, a price cut teaches your best clients to wait for November. Add calls, access, templates or a longer term instead.
