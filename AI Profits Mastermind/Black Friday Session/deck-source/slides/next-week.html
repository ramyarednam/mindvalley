Bring these four things

Know your number before you write a single email.

1 · Your buckets

How many never bought, bought once, current clients.

2 · Your track

Low ticket, high ticket, or both.

3 · Your deal

One deal type, plus your real limit.

4 · Your number

Goal ÷ price = sales. Sales ÷ your conversion = waitlist.

Example: $30,000 ÷ $1,500 = 20 sales. At 5% (use your own past rate), you need 400 on the waitlist by Nov 15.
