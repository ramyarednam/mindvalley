02 · Offer framing

If you sell both

Low ticket on Black Friday. High ticket to the people who just said yes.

Black Friday: low ticket

Open to everyone. Clear deal, short window. This is your volume.

Early access: high ticket

Past clients and hot leads only. Bonuses, deposit or payment plan, a call to close.

December: ascend

Invite low-ticket buyers to the high-ticket offer, with a January start.
