October vs November

October earns the right to sell in November.

October: value, no discount

  • “3 mistakes keeping you stuck at $10K a month”
  • A client story carousel
  • A free live training → waitlist

November: proof, then offer

  • Client wins with names and numbers
  • “Why I only open this once a year”
  • 48-hour countdown Stories
