02 · Offer framing

Cyber Monday + real scarcity

Cyber Monday is a second launch, not a rerun.

Make Monday news

A different product, a 2-for-1, or a giftable version.

Real urgency

Seat caps you can defend. Bonuses that expire. Next year’s price rise.

Never

Timers that reset. “Last chance” five times.
