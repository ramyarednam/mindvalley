02 · Offer framing

Your offer in one sentence

If you can’t say it in one sentence, they won’t buy it.

For [who] who want [outcome] in [time]: [product]. This Black Friday only, [deal] + [bonus]. [real limit]. Ends [date].

“For coaches who want a business that runs without them in 6 months: the Scale Program. $1,500 (normally $2,000) + 3 private calls. 50 seats, because I coach every call. Ends Sunday Nov 29.”
