Ads: works vs burns

Prospect early. Retarget late. Never pay to learn in BF week.

Works

  • October: no-discount founder video, cold
  • Lead ads to fill the waitlist
  • BF week: warm audiences only
  • Fresh creative every 5–7 days

Burns money

  • Cold prospecting at peak prices
  • Generic “50% OFF!!!” graphics
  • Brand-new ads launched on the day
  • Cold traffic to a high-ticket checkout
