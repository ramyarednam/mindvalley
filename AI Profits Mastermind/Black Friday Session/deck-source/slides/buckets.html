02 · Offer framing

Three buckets, three offers

Your easiest sale is to someone who already paid you.

Never bought

Entry offer: a course, first program, payment plan.

Bought once

Comeback or cross-sell: the next step, plus a thank-you bonus.

Current clients

Upgrade: longer term, higher tier, add-on coaching.

Each bucket gets its own offer and its own email.
