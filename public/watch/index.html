Studio preview: this test is not live, so your viewing is not counted in quotas.

You are invited to watch an unreleased episode before anyone else. While you watch, your webcam helps us see which moments hold attention.

What we record

Whether a face is looking at the screen (yes or no), four times a second. When you press the spacebar or B. Your answers to a short survey at the end.

What we never record

Video or photos of you. The camera image is analysed on your own computer and never uploaded.

How long

The full episode, plus about 3 minutes for setup and survey. You can pause.

Confidential

This episode is not public. Please don't record, share or discuss it. Your viewer ID is shown on the video.

Signal data is kept for up to 90 days, then deleted; only totals across all viewers are kept. You can stop at any time by closing this tab.

A little about you

This helps us compare how different audiences react. It is not linked to your name.

Set up your camera

Sit about an arm's length from the screen, with light on your face (not behind you). Glasses are fine.

Starting camera…

Calibration shows 9 dots, one at a time. Look straight at each dot until it disappears. It takes about 15 seconds.

You're set up

  1. Press the spacebar whenever something is interesting, surprising or valuable. Press as often as you like.
  2. Press B if a part feels boring or confusing (optional).
  3. A few times, a message will ask you to press A. Please do it within 8 seconds.
  4. You can pause, but you can't skip ahead. Watch as you normally would.
