Why 2026 is lower

Campaign revenue, 2026 above 2025, month by month

2026 (solid)2025 (hatched)
November 2025 is Black Friday: $6.4M of membership orders tagged to the sale, $7.7M of campaign revenue in the month. The 2026 Black Friday runs Nov 16-30; presale orders so far are $0.58M.

Like-for-like (1 Jan to 10 Nov) campaign revenue fell from $43.2M to $29.9M. Five things explain it.

01

No Future Human in 2026

−$4.7M

The January 2025 festival sold $1.15M of tickets, $2.42M of the Future Human Mastery bundle, $0.99M of Total Mastery and $0.16M of virtual passes. Nothing replaced it in 2026.

02

Manifesting Summit: in person became free

$6.69M → $4.03M

2026 had 136k sign-ups to 2025's 54k and still made $2.7M less: $124 per sign-up in 2025, $30 in 2026. Paid tickets were $1.0M of the 2025 number on their own.

03

AI Summit: July became March, with 50k fewer sign-ups

$7.08M → $5.07M

Both summits were free. 2025 ran in July after Manifesting had closed and pulled 170k sign-ups; 2026 ran in March, six weeks after Social Media, with 120k.

04

Certifications: one launch fewer, no Business Coach, no rolling intakes

$7.5M → $4.0M

2025 launched CLC (Feb, $1.28M), Business Coach (Apr, $0.71M) and Hypnotherapist (Sep, $2.10M) and kept monthly intakes worth $2.3M. 2026 ran CLC (Feb, $1.17M) and Hypnotherapist (Aug, $1.43M) and stopped the intakes.

05

Membership ran fewer campaigns

$5.9M → $4.1M

Jan to 10 Nov membership launch-tagged. 2025 had the Best of Mindvalley summit, two promos, the June renewal promo ($1.13M) and the Membership Summit. 2026 kept only the June promo ($0.70M). Social Media Summit also slipped, $3.23M to $2.10M, on six weeks of runway over Christmas.

06

What 2026 added

+$3.5M

Accelerators are a new line ($1.34M from five webinar-led launches). Expert to Authority ($2.89M) beat Entrepreneurship ($2.52M) and seeded Guilds. WildFit came back ($0.47M). AI for Founders is at $1.97M with the class not yet started.

The progression

Future Human is the identity. The year walks people through the four masteries that build it.

  1. Q1 · Build visibility, then clarity. Social Media Summit at 1BFS (Jan 8-10) and the Life Coach launch open the year. The Q1 challenge (Feb 15-21, clarity) runs the week the Manifesting campaign opens.
  2. Q2 · Build clarity, then systems. Manifesting Summit in person (Apr 16-18), Membership Summit (May 22-23). The Q2 challenge (Jun 1-7, systems) runs as the AI campaign opens.
  3. Q3 · Build systems, then authority. MVU Porto and the AI Summit with a paid ticket (Jul 23-25), Hypnotherapist cohort (Aug 2). The Q3 challenge (Aug 15-21, authority) runs inside the Expert to Authority campaign; the summit follows Sep 17-19.
  4. Q4 · Become it. AI for Founders (Oct 29-31), Future Human Dubai (Nov 5-7) where the four-mastery bundle is sold, then the Q4 challenge (Nov 16 - Dec 16) carrying the list into Black Friday, Lifebook and Q5.
What a $1M challenge needs. The best challenge on record, 7 Sacred States in Dec 2025, pulled 25.5k sign-ups and $0.43M of attributed orders, about $17 per sign-up. At that rate $1M is roughly 60k sign-ups; the alternative is a paid product sold at the end of the challenge (the next summit's VIP ticket or an accelerator), which is why each challenge is placed at the start of a summit campaign.
