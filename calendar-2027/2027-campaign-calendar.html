How it works

Three rules

  1. Only launch campaigns count. Revenue on this page is from orders that carry a Product Launch tag at checkout: summits, cert launches, Black Friday, Q5, quest webinars. Evergreen membership, programs, class starts and States are not campaign revenue.
  2. Month by month, as in the official calendar. Each month lists the calendar's campaigns, what each made, the month's campaign total and the month's total revenue from every order.
  3. 2027 target = 30% above what the same campaign made in 2026. If it did not run in 2026, 30% above 2025. Challenges and the Porto accelerators are $1M each on your instruction.
One thing to know about the tags: about 45% of Academy launch orders (deposits, payment plans) carry no launch tag, so a $1.17M cert cohort shows as $0.27M here. The tables say so where it matters. Tagging every checkout would lift reported campaign revenue by roughly $6M a year without changing a sale.

Like-for-like (1 Jan to 10 Nov) campaign revenue fell from $26.2M to $18.9M. In campaign terms, five things explain it.

01

AI Summit: hybrid in July became free in March

$6.25M → $3.23M

Launch-tagged AI Mastery orders, Jul-Aug 2025 vs Mar-Apr 2026. 170k sign-ups in 2025 vs 120k; paid tickets and a July slot after the Manifesting cohort. The biggest single swing of the year.

02

No Future Human in 2026

−$2.44M

January 2025's festival sold $1.57M of the Future Human Mastery bundle, $0.71M of Total Mastery and $0.16M of virtual passes, all tagged to the event. 2026 had no January festival and no bundle.

03

Manifesting Summit: in person became free

$3.45M → $2.15M

Apr-Jun tagged orders. 2026 had 136k sign-ups to 2025's 54k and still made $1.3M less: $124 per sign-up in 2025, $30 in 2026.

04

Membership ran fewer campaigns

$5.9M → $4.1M

Jan-10 Nov membership launch-tagged. 2025 had the Best of Mindvalley summit (Feb), two promos (Jan, Apr-May), the June renewal promo ($1.07M) and the Membership Summit (Aug, $0.42M). 2026 kept only the June promo ($0.62M). The 2027 plan keeps one Membership Summit and drops every promo except Black Friday and Q5, as you asked.

05

Certifications: one launch fewer, no Business Coach

$1.45M → $1.06M

Tagged cert orders Jan-10 Nov. 2025: CLC Feb, Business Coach Apr, Hypnotherapist Sep plus deposits through the year. 2026: CLC Feb and Hypnotherapist Aug. Business Coach is dropped for 2027 on your call.
