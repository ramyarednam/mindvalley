Part 1

Why 2026 is lower, in order of size

Like-for-like (1 Jan to 10 Nov), campaign revenue is $43.0M to $29.8M, a $13.2M gap. Six things explain almost all of it.

01

The membership base shrank

$23.5M → $9.9M

New-member revenue, Jan to 10 Nov. New orders fell from 12-18k a month to 5-7k while evergreen masterclass sign-ups rose 20%. Fewer members means a smaller warm audience for every summit (members were 35-45% of summit sign-ups) and a smaller Black Friday base. Not a calendar problem, but it caps every membership promo.

02

Two flagship summits went free

−$4.6M

Manifesting: $6.69M hybrid in 2025 (54k leads, $124 per sign-up) to $4.03M free in 2026 (136k leads, $30). AI: $7.08M hybrid in July 2025 to $5.07M free in March 2026. More leads, less revenue. Paid tickets are the single biggest lever in the plan.

03

Certifications halved

$7.5M → $4.0M

2025 ran CLC, Business Coach and Hypnotherapist launches plus monthly rolling cohorts worth about $2.3M. 2026 ran two launches (CLC Feb $1.17M, MCH Aug $1.43M), skipped Business Coach and stopped the monthly intakes.

04

No Future Human in 2026

−$4.7M

January 2025's festival sold $1.15M of tickets, $2.42M of the Future Human Mastery bundle and $0.99M of Total Mastery. Nothing replaced it in 2026; Total Mastery alone did $0.46M.

05

Social Media and Speaking slipped

−$1.6M

Social Media at 1BFS Dubai doubled sign-ups to 145k but made $2.10M vs $3.23M, with only six weeks of runway and $0.48M refunded. Speaking & Influence $2.04M to $1.53M.

06

What 2026 added

+$2.2M

Accelerators are a new line ($1.34M from five webinar-led launches). Expert to Authority beat Entrepreneurship ($2.76M vs $2.52M) and seeded Guilds ($0.13M). WildFit came back ($0.47M). MVU 2027 presale is ahead of MVU 2026's pace.

Part 4

What the two years tell us to keep, fix and drop

  1. Charge for the room. Hybrid summits out-earn free ones by 30-65% on the same product. Jaideep's plan already makes Manifesting in-person and AI paid-ticket; the data supports both and says do the same for Expert to Authority.
  2. Sixty days of runway, and never two summits on the list at once. The best launches (AI Jul 2025, Manifesting May 2025) had 45-60 days; Social Media at 1BFS had six weeks over Christmas.
  3. Keep the VIP upgrade on every summit. 22,000 upgrades in 2026 made $0.87M at almost no cost, and VIP buyers are the mastery shortlist.
  4. Four certification launches plus monthly intakes. That cadence is worth about $8M; two launches with no intakes is worth $4M.
  5. Future Human is the identity and the biggest single ticket. Sell the four-mastery bundle at the event, as in January 2025.
  6. Two membership summits a year. Feb and Aug 2025 pulled 75k leads and $1.5M attributed; 2026 ran neither, and new-member revenue fell 58%.
  7. Accelerators are the cheap filler between summits. Webinar-led, $0.3-0.6M each, no summit needed. Six in 2027.
  8. Q4 is one machine. Free summit in late November, Black Friday, challenge, Lifebook webinar, Q5: the 2025 sequence made $12M+ in eight weeks. Keep the order.
  9. Drop or merge what did not move the number. Masters of Manifesting seminar, Windstar cruise, the Jan features promo and the one-day celebrity accelerators without a product in Metabase.
