How the 2027 calendar was built

Only launch campaigns count. Every number on this page is revenue from orders that carry a Product Launch tag at checkout, pulled from Metabase. Evergreen membership, programs, class starts and quest releases are left out. Where a product never carries a tag (MVU tickets, States on Shopify, event tickets) the product's own sales in the campaign window are used and the row says so. 2027 targets are 30% above what the same campaign did in 2026.

Why 2026 is lower

Launch-tagged revenue, 2026 above 2025, month by month

2026 (solid)2025 (hatched)
November 2025 is Black Friday: $7.2M of launch-tagged orders in one month, $6.5M of it membership. Nothing in 2026 comes close yet; the 2026 Black Friday runs Nov 16-30.

Like-for-like (1 Jan to 10 Nov) campaign revenue fell from $26.2M to $18.9M. In campaign terms, five things explain it.

01

AI Summit: July became March, with 50k fewer sign-ups

$6.29M → $3.25M

Launch-tagged AI Mastery orders, Jul-Aug 2025 vs Mar-Apr 2026. Both summits were free. 2025 had 170k sign-ups and a July slot after the Manifesting cohort had closed; 2026 had 120k and ran six weeks after Social Media. The biggest single swing of the year.

02

No Future Human in 2026

−$2.44M

January 2025's festival sold $1.57M of the Future Human Mastery bundle, $0.71M of Total Mastery and $0.16M of virtual passes, all tagged to the event. 2026 had no January festival and no bundle.

03

Manifesting Summit: in person became free

$3.45M → $2.20M

Apr-Jun tagged orders. 2026 had 136k sign-ups to 2025's 54k and still made $1.3M less: $124 per sign-up in 2025, $30 in 2026.

04

Membership ran fewer campaigns

$5.9M → $4.1M

Jan-10 Nov membership launch-tagged. 2025 had the Best of Mindvalley summit (Feb, $0.36M in summit week), two promos (Jan, Apr-May), the June renewal promo ($1.13M) and the Membership Summit (Aug, $0.22M in summit week). 2026 kept only the June promo ($0.70M). The 2027 plan keeps one Membership Summit and drops every promo except Black Friday and Q5, as you asked.

05

Certifications: one launch fewer, no Business Coach

$1.45M → $1.06M

Tagged cert orders Jan-10 Nov. 2025: CLC Feb, Business Coach Apr, Hypnotherapist Sep plus deposits through the year. 2026: CLC Feb and Hypnotherapist Aug. Business Coach is dropped for 2027 on your call.

06

What 2026 added

+$1.9M

Accelerators ($0.40M AI, $0.09M AI Clones, $0.32M Wealth Code, $0.22M Priestley, $0.08M Manifesting Blocks), Expert to Authority ($2.42M vs Entrepreneurship $2.24M), WildFit back ($0.23M) and AI for Founders already at $1.22M with the class not yet started.
