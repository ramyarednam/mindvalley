## 5. Voice and rules

**Vishen's newsletter voice** (anything signed Vishen): cold open, no "Hi {{first_name}}", short lines, one idea per line, self-deprecating, specific, never salesy in the body, signed "Vishen". Subject lines over 35 characters outperform on his list. Humour is dry: "(I'm not judging.)" is the register.

**Mindvalley brand voice** (anything signed The Mindvalley Team): warm, confident, specific. Name the program, the teacher, the feature. No apologies, no "we've missed you" filler.

**Hard rules, every email:**
- No price in the body. Price and renewal terms live only in the compliance line at the bottom of the email.
- Compliance line, members: Lifetime price by tier, prorated for current subscribers, one payment, no renewal, deadline Monday night. Compliance line, non-members: $199 first year, regular $499, renews at $199 a year while the membership is active, 15-day refund, deadline Monday night.
- Never "all of Mindvalley". Say "every Quest, every new program, the full meditation library".
- "For life" only with its condition attached (Lifetime Membership, one payment).
- No fake scarcity. The 1,500 counter on the members page is real and can be referenced as "the counter on the page is real". Nothing else is limited.
- Never "lowest price of the year" or "biggest discount ever".
- Members' offer is Lifetime, never "upgrade to a discount". Call it Lifetime Membership. For members, say the price is calculated in Billing & Subscriptions with credit for unused time; don't quote it.
- Don't say "community". Don't address members as a crowd; one reader.
- One CTA per email in the main body. A PS can carry a second link.
- Buttons say what they do: [Get Lifetime], [See the offer], [Watch the video].
- Every link carries an otag. Pattern this year: email_campaign_<list>_<name>, e.g. email_campaign_mvm_email1, email_campaign_daily_email1, email_campaign_mvm_vlnl_nov18. Lists: mvm (members), daily, highlights, weekly (non-members), coach, vlnl (Vishen's newsletter list), chumvm (churned).

**Lists:** non-members (daily, highlights, weekly), members (mvm), churned (folded into non-members), coach, Vishen's newsletter list. Members and non-members always get separate versions when the offer appears, because the offer is different.

## 7. Last year, for calibration

2025 ran "50% off for life" for non-members and a 3-year deal for members. Best performers: the non-member announcement ($91k from 1.55M delivered), the 48-hours-left email ($90k), the members' extended-24-hours email ($108k from 208k delivered), and the waitlist emails (42 to 46% open, $31 to $35 revenue per click). Short, specific urgency emails beat long ones. The Vishen newsletter in the middle of the week made the least money but kept open rates up for the rest of the sequence.
