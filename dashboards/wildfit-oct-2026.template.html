How these numbers are built

Live data. Every number on this page comes straight from Metabase when the page opens, using your own Metabase connection. Refresh data re-runs every query. Sign-ups and orders come from MV BigQuery. Sessions come from GA4 (l2_user_behavior.ga4_web_session_event), read live so today shows up without the daily table's two-day lag. Each source shows its last update time at the top.

Live webinar only, all languages. Sign-ups come from l3_leads.fact_lead on each launch's live webinar page and its language versions: /wildfit/transformation90 for Jul 2023 and Jul 2024, /wildfit/health90 for Mar 2025 and /wildfit/invite for Oct 2026. Masterclass pages are never counted.

Sign-ups are people, not form submits. Each person counts once per launch, on the day of their first sign-up, from promo start to 7 days after the webinar. Mindvalley staff accounts are removed. Members means an active membership at the moment of sign-up.

Sessions are GA4 visits that loaded the webinar page, thank-you and replay pages excluded. Visitors who declined analytics cookies send no session ID, so they're counted from their session-start ping. Cookie-declined visits weren't recorded before 2024, so Jul 2023 sessions run slightly low and its sign-up rate slightly high. Sign-up rate is sign-ups ÷ sessions. The headline compares complete days only, because the two sources refresh on different schedules during the day.

Launch dates. Promo start and webinar: 31 Jul → 2 Aug 2023 (webinar on Day 3), 27 Jul → 30 Jul 2024 (Day 4), 5 Mar → 11 Mar 2025 (Day 7), 30 Sep → 6 Oct 2026 (Day 7). Sign-ups before a promo start are left out.

Revenue. Orders from l3_sales.fact_sales_order for the WildFit programme (every language) plus anything sold through a WildFit funnel. The Mar 2025 webinar sold Membership through the WildFit funnel, so it is included. Each order counts once by order_id. Membership renewals are left out. Revenue before a promo start counts as masterclass and is left out. The Jul 2023 and Jul 2024 webinars ran while the masterclass cart was still open, so launch-window revenue in those years includes buyers who never registered for the webinar; the launch table shows how much came from registrants.

Channels. Channels follow the team's lead-source report and are set by utm_source. Email: every email_campaign_ send, split by email. Platform: in-app, app and website banners and newsfeed. Ads: anything paid; Paid social (Meta) is facebook-paid, facebook and other Meta ads, Paid search (Google) is google and google-paid. Socials: organic social posts, broadcasts, chatbots, LinkedIn, YouTube and Substack. WhatsApp: WhatsApp community links. Organic: blog, AI search, affiliates, onboarding journeys and other referrals. Undefined: no usable utm_source. Sign-up channel comes from the sign-up's tracking tags; revenue channel from l3_sales.fact_sales_attribution.

Differences are 2026 minus the past launch, as a percentage of the past launch, for the same campaign day. Green means 2026 is higher, red means lower. Days are UTC, which is 8am in Malaysia.
