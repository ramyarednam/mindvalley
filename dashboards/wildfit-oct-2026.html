How these numbers are built

Live webinar only. Sign-ups come from l3_leads.fact_lead on each launch's live webinar page, in every language: /wildfit/transformation90 for Jul 2023 and Jul 2024, /wildfit/health90 for Mar 2025 and /wildfit/invite for Oct 2026, including /es/, /de/ and other language versions. Masterclass pages (/wildfit/masterclass, /wildfit/content) are never counted.

Sign-ups are people, not form submits. Each person counts once per launch, on their first sign-up from promo start to 7 days after the webinar. Mindvalley staff accounts are removed. Members means an active membership at the moment of sign-up.

Launch dates. Promo start and webinar: 31 Jul → 2 Aug 2023, 27 Jul → 30 Jul 2024, 5 Mar → 11 Mar 2025, 30 Sep → 6 Oct 2026. Sign-ups on a webinar page before its promo start are left out.

Revenue. Orders from l3_sales.fact_sales_order for the WildFit programme (every language) plus anything sold through a WildFit funnel. The Mar 2025 webinar sold Membership through the WildFit funnel, so it is included. Each order counts once by order_id. Membership renewals in the window are left out because they aren't new sales. Gross revenue is order_amount. Refunds are refunded to date.

Masterclass vs live webinar. Revenue before a launch's promo start counts as masterclass and is left out of launch revenue. The Jul 2023 and Jul 2024 webinars ran while the masterclass cart was still open, so launch-window revenue in those years includes buyers who never registered for the webinar. The "bought by webinar registrants" row shows the part that came from people on the webinar list.

Channels. Email covers every Braze or newsletter send. Platform is the Mindvalley app, mindvalley.com banners and internal links. Paid ads is paid social and paid search. Other / untagged is traffic with no usable tracking tag (plus SMS and Telegram for sign-ups). Revenue channel comes from l3_sales.fact_sales_attribution. Never add the channel figures to other reports' totals.

Days are UTC, which is 8am in Malaysia. This page is a snapshot with no live connection. Every figure carries the pull time at the top. To update it, re-run scripts/wildfit-launch.sql and republish to the same link.
