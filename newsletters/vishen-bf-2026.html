Personal Growth Was Never Personal. Until Now.

By Vishen Lakhiani

Here's an uncomfortable truth about the industry I've given my life to.

For about fifty years, personal growth hasn't actually been personal.

A bestselling book prints the same words for a million readers. A seminar speaks the same sentences to three thousand people in a hotel ballroom.

Even at Mindvalley, when we record a meditation, millions of people hear the exact same track. Whether they're grieving. Falling in love. Or lying awake at 3am worrying about money.

We took the most personal journey a human being can go on…

…and we mass-produced it.

Now don't get me wrong. It worked. Mass-produced wisdom has changed millions of lives. Including mine.

But I always knew it was a compromise.

Think about the best teacher you ever had. They didn't give you the same answer they gave the person before you. They listened. They remembered. They met you exactly where you were.

That was the one thing you could never scale.

Until this year.

The year the compromise ended

I turned 50 this year. And I can honestly tell you, I haven't felt this alive as a builder since the day I started Mindvalley.

Because something happened to technology in 2026 that most people still haven't grasped.

AI stopped being a tool you type questions into.

It became something that can learn you.

Two weeks ago, at our AI for Founders Summit, I said something to a room of entrepreneurs that I keep coming back to:

"You don't need a hundred AI tools. You need one AI that learns you."

And when I applied that one idea to personal growth, everything I'd dreamed about for years suddenly became buildable. Not in years. In weeks.

So that's what I've been doing. Building. Obsessively. On walks, on flights, on road trips with my kids. I record ideas as voice notes, hand them to my AI agents, and wake up to see what they've built.

Here's what's come out of it. I don't think I've ever been more excited to share something with you.

1. Omvana, the meditation app I've dreamed about for years

Omvana is my dream app.

In 2023, we hired an agency to rebuild it. It fell short. This year, I rebuilt it myself in a single week, mostly by speaking to AI over WhatsApp. At one point we shipped 15 test versions in 24 hours.

Here's what makes it different from every meditation app you've tried.

It doesn't just hand you a library and wish you luck. It holds nearly 1,500 meditations from the world's greatest teachers. But it also listens.

Tell it what you're facing. A hard conversation tomorrow. A goal you're chasing. A loss you're carrying. And it can create a meditation for exactly that, in the voice you choose.

Here's a small story that tells you everything about how this was built.

One morning I noticed some tracks were louder than others. So I left my AI agent a WhatsApp message and went down for breakfast. By my first cup of coffee, it had analysed all 1,497 tracks and built a tool to balance every single one.

That's the level of care that's now possible.

For the first time, meditation can be made for one person. You.

2. Body CEO, a trainer that knows your blood

When I turned 50, I set myself a slightly absurd goal: to be in better shape at 60 than I was at 21.

Today, I'm in the best shape of my life. Body CEO is the app I built to get there.

It reads your bloodwork and your doctor's notes. It tracks your supplements and tells you which ones are actually working. Training for HYROX or a Spartan race? It builds the plan.

And my favourite part: walk into any hotel gym in the world, take a photo, and it designs your workout around the equipment in that room.

It's guided by teachers like Ben Greenfield and Gary Brecka. I built the first version in three days. It launches soon.

3. EVE, who now remembers you everywhere

This is the piece that ties it all together.

EVE, our AI companion, now runs on something we call True Memory. Which means she's with you across every Mindvalley app.

What you learned in a Quest. What you're meditating on in Omvana. What you're working on with your body. One AI that knows the whole you. Not a dozen apps that each know a sliver.

That's what a great teacher always did.

They remembered you.

4. Finer Minds, because AI can't hug you

Personalisation solves half the problem. The other half is people.

Finer Minds is the community platform I built for our members. It's where they share what's working, organise meetups, and find each other. Whenever I travel to teach, I invite our students to join me for a meal. Last month in New York, I sat with a group of them over dinner, and it reminded me why I do this.

AI can learn you. Only people can hold you.

Humans still want humans.

Why this matters more to me than anything I've built

Mindvalley's mission has always been to touch one billion lives.

And here's what this year taught me about that number.

You can't transform a billion people with one-size-fits-all. Real transformation has only ever happened one person at a time. A teacher. A student. A moment of being truly seen.

For the first time in history, technology lets us do "one person at a time"… a billion times over.

That's why I'm building at 50 like I'm 25.

And the irony? The biggest gift AI has given me isn't a product. It's my life back. I just spent a week with my son at his university in Florida. His sister flew in from London. No boardrooms. No meetings. Just us.

That's what all of this is for.
