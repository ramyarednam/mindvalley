# Omvana Launch: 3 Talking-Head Scripts for Vishen (v3)

**Brand idea running through all three:** *Omvana knows you.* Every video ends on the end card **"Omvana knows you."** with the waitlist URL.

**Who we're talking to:** this comes from the positioning brief's waitlist data. 4 in 5 are women, 4 in 5 are 45+, and 1 in 3 is 65+. They arrive with a feeling, not a technique. They're tired, they look after everyone else, and they wonder if they're enough. Talk to *one* person, warmly. Vishen stays playful and confident, but it's **healing energy, not performance.**

**Set-up:** Vishen seated, one camera at chest height, phone in hand for demo moments. Teleprompter. He's showing a friend something he's genuinely excited about, for the first time.

**Social cut:** 9:16 vertical with burned-in captions, built to work muted. Jump-cut every 3–5s. Screen recordings go in as cutaways while Vishen keeps talking. The iPhone build screens in the brief (Home, Pick, Mixer, Sounds, Create, Consent) are ready-made cutaways.

**How to read the scripts**
- Plain lines are what Vishen says.
- `[ON SCREEN]` is caption text for the editor.
- `[CUTAWAY]` is footage over his voice.
- `[VISHEN: ...]` is a personal detail only he can fill in.
- `[beat]` means pause.

**Words to leave out** (from the brief): optimise, hack, crush, level up, beat, #1, smarter than, dominate, AI-powered, fix yourself, cure.
**Words that fit:** gentle, held, heard, remember, rest, come back, soften, let go, for you, today, safe, enough.

**Sources:** the Omvana positioning brief (Nov 12, iPhone build), the omvana.com/waitlist page, the revised waitlist/membership artifact, and Omvana history from the 2019 PRD and data deck in Drive.
