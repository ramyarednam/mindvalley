# Omvana Launch: 3 Talking-Head Scripts for Vishen (v2)

**Set-up for all three:** Vishen seated, one camera at chest height, phone in hand for demo moments. Read from the teleprompter and talk like he's showing a friend something he just built. Overall feel: *"I've never shown this to anyone. Look at this."*

**Social cut:** 9:16 vertical with burned-in captions. Most people watch muted, so the on-screen text has to carry the hook. Jump-cut every 3–5 seconds and put screen recordings in as cutaways while Vishen keeps talking.

**How to read the scripts**
- Plain lines are what Vishen says.
- `[ON SCREEN]` is bold caption text for the editor.
- `[CUTAWAY]` is footage to cut in over his voice.
- `[VISHEN: ...]` is a personal detail only he can fill in. These make the video feel real, so don't skip them.
- `[beat]` means pause, let it land.

**Copy source:** the live omvana.com/waitlist page and the membership page copy from the revised waitlist artifact. Product details in these scripts are taken from that copy.
